# UX profile: dreaminreality.co.uk

- **Aesthetic reference:** Custom, "blueprint dark" (nearest archetype: Linear). Navy canvas, slate panels with 1px hairlines, one cyan accent, no shadows. The look comes from the three.js service scenes.
- **Banned:** glassmorphism and `backdrop-filter`; pastel pill badges on static metadata; decorative gradients and neon glows; rainbow CTAs; cartoon empty-state people; nested scrollbars; decorative icons inside inputs.
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.1 AA. 44px touch targets. Visible focus ring (`.focus-ring`) on every interactive element.
- **Voice:** Utilitarian and minimal. British English. Nouns and verbs. See `CLAUDE.md` for banned words.
- **Icons:** project SVGs in `public/images`, inline only. One set. No emoji as UI icons.
- **Type:** Poppins, weights 300 (body, headings) and 600 (keywords, eyebrows, buttons) on dark pages. 12px is for eyebrows and chips only.
- **Motion:** 100/150/200/300ms, ease-out in, `transform` and `opacity` only, no decorative loops. Scroll-scrubbed scenes (`ScrollScene`) are exempt from duration tokens: the reader's scroll is the clock. The homepage route light is a timed scene: 1.2s forward, 0.6s back. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` scenes are replaced by still frames.
